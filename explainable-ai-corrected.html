What is Explainable AI?

Explainable AI (XAI) is a collection of methods and practices intended to make AI behavior more understandable to people. For large language models, the challenge is not simply explaining an answer—it is determining whether an explanation actually reflects the internal process that produced it.

Trust

Users need evidence for when model outputs deserve confidence and when they require verification.

Accountability

Transparent evaluation and documentation help organizations investigate failures and establish responsibility.

Debugging

Interpretability can reveal patterns, representations, or pathways associated with unexpected behavior.

Responsible Use

Testing, oversight, documentation, and monitoring help translate technical performance into safer deployment decisions.

Explainability + Validation + Monitoring = Stronger Evidence for Trust

A model does not become trustworthy because it can produce a convincing explanation. Stronger accountability comes from combining interpretability research with repeatable evaluations, adversarial testing, human judgment, documentation, and monitoring after deployment.
