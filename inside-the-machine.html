01 / The Training Pipeline

Six stages. One model.

Training is not one giant step. It is a loop of collection, preparation, learning, alignment, evaluation, and deployment—each with its own demands and failure points.

01

Data Collection

Scrape, license, and assemble text, code, image, and other corpora.

02

Preprocessing

Clean, deduplicate, filter for quality and safety, then tokenize.

03

Pretraining

Learn statistical patterns across massive token sequences.

04

Fine-Tuning / RLHF

Shape behavior toward instructions and human preferences.

05

Evaluation & Safety

Benchmark performance and red-team for risk, bias, and failure.

06

Deployment

Optimize inference and serve the model to users at scale.

07 / Shelby's Takeaway

AI isn't intangible.

A prompt goes in and an answer appears seconds later. The infrastructure behind that moment is anything but instant.

Studying the training process changed how I think about generative AI. Behind a model response is an enormous physical and financial system involving data, GPUs, electricity, human feedback, engineering time, evaluation, and millions of dollars.

What stood out to me most was not simply how expensive AI is to build, but how tightly every resource is connected. More data demands more compute. More compute demands more energy. Hardware, energy, and time drive cost. And none of those resources guarantee a stronger model unless the underlying data, training strategy, alignment, and evaluation are effective.

For me, understanding AI means looking beyond the interface. The real system begins long before the first user ever types a prompt.
