KEY TAKEAWAYS

What the visual experiments made clear.

01

Data matters.

Noise can turn a nearly perfect classifier into a much less certain model.

02

Architecture matters.

A model needs enough representational capacity to match the complexity of the problem.

03

Visualization matters.

Watching decision boundaries change makes neural network learning easier to understand.

“A successful neural network depends on data quality, data complexity, and an architecture with enough capacity to learn the pattern.”
